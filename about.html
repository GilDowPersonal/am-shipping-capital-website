About this concept.

A proposed visual direction for a conversation, not an official company website.

Prepared for a meeting, this presentation explores a proposed website and AI direction for Am Shipping Capital. Herzliya, Israel is the location provided for this concept. The business's legal identity, connection to that location, and official details have not been independently verified.

The home page presents a proposed company-facing maritime identity. Its AI concept opens a dedicated exploration of automation, AI assistance, and bounded agents across shipping workflows, while retaining the original photography, palette, and gradual scroll reveals.

The AI applications are proposals to evaluate, not claims about installed systems or verified company services. No company systems are connected and no live agents run through this website.

The AI & automation concept describes eight applications, possible integration points, approval boundaries, and a draft-only pilot. The actual business processes, data permissions, software interfaces, and success criteria would need to be established with the team before implementation.

A proposed visual identity

A maritime palette.

Deep navy, warm paper, and coastal accents create a calm setting for large-scale shipping photography. These are colors to review, not company-approved standards.

Navy#071A2B
Paper#F7F5F0
Teal#087F8C
Seafoam#B8E4DA
Slate#536572

Navy anchors the layout; Paper creates breathing room. Teal adds a restrained accent, Seafoam highlights actions, and Slate supports secondary text on light surfaces.

Keeping the distinction clear

What this concept does not claim.

No independently verified company testimonials are included in this presentation. This is not a claim that no reviews exist. Praise for unrelated businesses has not been attributed to Am Shipping Capital.

No official contact details, company history, service guarantees, or partnerships are asserted. The concept does not accept bookings or shipment requests. Its purpose is to make the proposed visual identity and AI integration opportunities tangible for discussion, without inventing operational results or savings.

Any future official presentation would need verified identity, accurate business information, and approval of its branding and claims before publication.
